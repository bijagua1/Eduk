# US Market Strategy for Eduk

To ensure **Eduk** succeeds in the US market, we must align with the local educational standards and parental expectations.

## Educational Alignment
Eduk will align its question engine with the **Common Core State Standards (CCSS)** for Mathematics and English Language Arts, as well as the **Next Generation Science Standards (NGSS)**.

| Subject | Focus Areas | US Standards |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mathematics** | Algebra, Geometry, Statistics, and Logic | Common Core (CCSS.MATH) |
| **ELA** | Reading Comprehension, Vocabulary, and Grammar | Common Core (CCSS.ELA) |
| **Science** | Physical, Life, and Earth Sciences | NGSS |
| **Social Studies** | US History, Civics, and World Geography | State-specific Standards |

## Parental Controls & Privacy (COPPA)
Privacy is paramount. Eduk will be built with **COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act)** compliance in mind.

- **Zero Data Selling**: No student data will ever be sold to third parties.
- **Parental Consent**: All data collection (even for AI processing) requires explicit parental verification via the manager's account.
- **Secure Reporting**: Reports sent to parents will be encrypted and accessible only through the authenticated manager app.

## User Experience (UX)
The interface will follow a "Modern-Pro" aesthetic, avoiding overly childish designs to ensure older students (middle and high school) don't feel patronized while using the app.

- **Dark Mode Support**: Standard for modern US apps.
- **Accessibility**: Full support for screen readers and high-contrast modes.
- **Incentive Systems**: Gamification elements like "Streaks" and "Badges" to encourage learning beyond the minimum required for unlocking.
